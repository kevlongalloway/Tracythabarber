The essence of cutting

One Head
At A Time.

No rushing the line, no guessing on the blend. The cut leaves the chair finished.

Coils, waves, locs, twists, low fades and high tops all get read differently. The shape is built around your hairline and your growth pattern, not forced into somebody else's template. Line-ups get the patience they deserve, and the beard gets connected the right way.

Bring a picture or bring nothing at all — you'll get told straight up what will actually sit right on your head.

  • Precision line-ups

    Straight edges, clean corners, no chewed hairline.

  • Every texture

    4C coils to starter locs to loose curls — all welcome in the chair.

  • All ages

    First haircuts to grown-man grooming. Kids are handled with patience.

  • Always on call

    Need to squeeze in before an event? Call or text and ask.
